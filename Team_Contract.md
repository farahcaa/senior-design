This contract sets how we divide work, communicate, and make decisions. All project work and discussion is recorded on GitHub so that progress and contributions are visible to the team and the instructor.

All three members are full-stack developers. Nobody is limited to one part of the system: work is divided by feature, not by layer. The backlog owner writes and prioritizes issues but takes work from the backlog like everyone else.

## 2. Backlog and Task Workflow

1. **The backlog lives in GitHub Issues** (with a GitHub Projects board). The backlog owner creates and prioritizes issues.
2. **Issues are written as user-facing features**, in user-story form ("As a ___, I want to ___, so that ___"), with acceptance criteria and links to related stories or design items (e.g., US-01, I5). Each issue is a complete vertical slice: whoever takes it builds it end to end, across the frontend, backend, and database.
3. **Taking work:** members pick issues they can realistically finish, assign themselves before starting, and move the issue to *In Progress*. Each member is expected to have at least one issue assigned to them every week.
4. **Doing work:** work happens on a branch named after the issue (e.g., `12-search-endpoint`). The pull request references the issue (`Closes #12`).
5. **Reporting:** progress, blockers, and questions are posted as comments on the issue, not only in private messages, so there is one record of who did what.
6. **Done:** an issue is done when its pull request is merged after review by at least one other member, and its acceptance criteria are met.

## 3. Communication and Response Times

- **Official channel:** GitHub issue and pull-request comments. Quick coordination may happen in group chat, but decisions and status updates must be copied to the relevant issue.
- **Requests for input:** when a member needs input, they @mention the teammate on the issue with a clear question and a deadline.
- **Response window:** members respond to @mentions within **48 hours** (72 hours over weekends and breaks).
- **No response:** if no response arrives within the window, the requester may proceed with a reasonable decision. They post a comment on the issue saying what they decided and that the teammate did not respond in time. The teammate may still raise concerns afterward, but work does not stop waiting.
- **Weekly check-in:** each week the backlog owner opens a new thread in the repository's **GitHub Discussions** under the *Weekly Check-ins* category. By **Sunday 11:59 PM** each member replies with what they finished, what they are working on, and any blockers. The threads are public to anyone with access to the repository, including the instructor, and are timestamped.

## 4. Deadlines and Workload

- Assignment work is due to the team **48 hours before** the course deadline so it can be reviewed and combined.
- If a member cannot finish an assigned issue, they say so on the issue as early as possible and unassign themselves so someone else can take it.
- Each member is expected to contribute a fair share of commits, reviews, and documentation over the semester. GitHub history (issues, commits, pull requests, reviews) is the record of contribution.

## 5. Decision-Making

- Design and technical decisions are proposed on an issue. Members have the response window to comment.
- Decisions are made by agreement of the members who respond. If members disagree, the member who took the issue decides and records the reasoning on the issue. Lasting design decisions are added to the decision log in `Design_Diagrams`.

## 6. Accountability and Escalation

If a member repeatedly misses deadlines or does not respond:

1. **First occurrence:** a reminder comment on the issue.
2. **Second occurrence:** the team discusses it directly and agrees on a plan, recorded on a GitHub issue.
3. **Continued problems:** the team informs the instructor, using the GitHub record as evidence.

## 7. Agreement

By signing (or by commenting "I agree" on the team-contract issue), each member agrees to follow this contract. It may be amended by agreement of all members, with the change recorded in the repository.
